WHAT THE PROBES WILL NOT TELL YOU
  Both probes stay green while FMS is unreachable and while Kafka is down. That
  is deliberate -- a dead dependency must not cascade into pod restarts. Watch
  these counters instead:
    vp_interceptor_upstream_connect_failed   FMS refusing or timing out
    vp_interceptor_kafka_delivery_failed     broker rejecting or unreachable
    vp_interceptor_tee_dropped               tee queue full, frames lost
    vp_interceptor_framer_desyncs            framing config does not match the wire
{{- if not .Values.config.kafka.enabled }}

NOTE: Kafka is disabled. VP<->FMS traffic is forwarded normally but nothing is
captured.
